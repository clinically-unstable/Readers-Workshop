Dante Alighieri

Divine Comedy

Dante Alighieri was an Italian writer and poet born on 1265, in Florence, Italy

At a time when Latin was the dominant language of literature, Dante broke tradition by writing in the Florence dialect

Furthermore, Divine Comedy helped establish a widespread standardized Italian language.

However, Dante was deeply controversial in his lifetime. He fiercely criticized the Catholic Church, and placed multiple popes in the Inferno (Hell) due to corrpution. Infact, many people mentioned in the Inferno were just historical figures that Dante disliked.

In 1302, Pope Boniface VIII banished him from Florence and threatened Dante with execution. In response, Boniface was put in the Eighth Circle of Hell.

Dante's work remains a cornerstone of Italian literature and withstood the test of time to this day.

The Divine Comedy is an epic poem written by Dante Alighieri, describing the journey through the three realms of the afterlife: Hell, Purgatory, and Heaven.

The poem is divided into three parts: Inferno, Purgatorio, and Paradiso.

Inferno is the first part of the Divine Comedy, and it describes Dante's journey through Hell, guided by the Roman poet Virgil.

In the Inferno, Dante encounters various sinners and witnesses their punishments, which are often symbolic of the sins they committed in life.

The Divine Comedy is considered one of the greatest works of world literature and has had a profound influence on Western culture and literature.

Overall, the Divine Comedy is a complex and multi-layered work that explores themes of morality, justice, and the human condition.

The Nine Circles of Hell

In Dante's poem, each circle punishes a different sin. The deeper the journey, the more severe the betrayal.

  1. I LimboVirtuous non-Christians and the unbaptized
  2. II LustThose swept away by desire
  3. III GluttonyThose ruled by excess
  4. IV GreedThe hoarders and the squanderers
  5. V WrathThe wrathful and the sullen
  6. VI HeresyThose who denied the soul's immortality
  7. VII ViolenceViolence against others, self, or God
  8. VIII FraudDeceivers in the ten bolge
  9. IX TreacheryBetrayers frozen in Cocytus

At the center of the frozen ninth circle waits Lucifer.
