Circle VI: Heresy

Those who denied the soul's immortality

Beyond the walls of Dis, heretics are confined to blazing tombs in a vast cemetery. Their graves will be sealed after the Last Judgment.

The souls

Dante focuses on those who taught that the soul dies with the body, including followers of Epicurus.

Their punishment

The dead lie in open stone tombs engulfed in flames. Their tombs will be sealed after the Last Judgment.

The place

The flaming burial ground lies within the fortified city of Dis. Its gates were closed to the travelers until a heavenly messenger intervened.

Dante's encounter

The Florentine noble Farinata degli Uberti rises from a tomb and speaks proudly with Dante about the rival factions of Florence.

Farinata and Cavalcante

Farinata speaks about the pride and divisions of Florence while Cavalcante dei Cavalcanti asks why Dante is there. Their contrasting family concerns reveal the power of earthly loyalties.

Knowledge in Hell

The heretics can perceive distant future events but cannot know the present. Dante is warned that this limited knowledge will end completely after the Last Judgment.
