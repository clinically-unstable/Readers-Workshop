I

Circle I: Limbo

Limbo lies just within Hell's gate. Its residents lived virtuous lives but, in Dante's medieval theology, died without Christian baptism or before Christianity.

The souls

Unbaptized infants and virtuous pagans dwell here. Dante meets the poets Homer, Horace, Ovid, and Lucan, as well as the philosopher Aristotle.

Their fate

They suffer no bodily torment. Their punishment is an eternity without hope of seeing God, felt as longing and quiet sorrow.

The place

A green meadow and a noble castle offer a calm contrast to the torments deeper in Hell. The castle represents the learning and virtue of its inhabitants.

Dante's guide

Virgil, himself a pagan poet, lives in Limbo. He leads Dante onward through the circles of Hell.

Inside the castle

Dante enters a green meadow and a noble castle, where he meets celebrated poets and philosophers, including Homer, Horace, Ovid, Lucan, and Aristotle.
