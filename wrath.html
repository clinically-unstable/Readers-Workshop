Circle V: Wrath

The wrathful and the sullen

The fifth circle occupies the muddy marsh of the river Styx. The wrathful fight on its surface while the sullen lie beneath it.

The souls

The openly wrathful fight on the marsh's surface; the sullen, who smothered their anger, lie submerged beneath it.

Their punishment

The wrathful strike and bite one another in the muddy water. Below, the sullen gurgle a song of their buried resentment.

The keeper

Phlegyas ferries Dante and Virgil across the Styx. The city of Dis rises on the far bank, marking the threshold to lower Hell.

Dante's encounter

Dante recognizes the Florentine Filippo Argenti among the wrathful and rejects his attempt to climb aboard the boat.

Across the Styx

Phlegyas carries the travelers toward the walled city of Dis, whose gates block their way at the far side of the marsh.
