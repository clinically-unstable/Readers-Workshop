VIII

Circle VIII: Fraud

Malebolge is a rocky landscape of ten concentric ditches linked by bridges. Each bolgia holds a different kind of deliberate fraud.

The souls

Those who used reason and trust to deceive others are punished here. The circle's ten ditches distinguish different forms of fraud.

The ten ditches

They hold seducers and panderers; flatterers; simoniacs; fortune-tellers; corrupt officials; hypocrites; thieves; fraudulent advisers; sowers of discord; and falsifiers.

The landscape

Stone bridges cross the ditches toward a central well, connecting the ten separate bolge.

The descent

Geryon, a creature with an honest-looking face and a treacherous body, carries the travelers from the seventh circle into Malebolge.
