IV

Circle IV: Greed

Hoarders and spendthrifts are divided into opposing groups and forced to push heavy weights against one another around the circle.

The souls

Both those who clung too tightly to wealth and those who wasted it without measure are condemned here.

Their punishment

Each group rolls a great weight along a semicircle, meets the other group, and turns to push back the way it came.

The keeper

Plutus, a mythic figure associated with wealth, guards the circle. Virgil rebukes him so the travelers can pass.

Virgil on Fortune

Virgil explains that Fortune governs the changing distribution of worldly goods. The travelers hear this account as they pass the circle's opposing groups.
