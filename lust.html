II

Circle II: Lust

At the threshold of the deeper circles, Minos judges each soul and assigns its place. The lustful are swept endlessly through a violent, dark storm.

The souls

Those who let passion overpower reason are punished here. Dante encounters the lovers Paolo Malatesta and Francesca da Rimini.

Their punishment

Unrelenting winds sweep the souls through darkness, leaving them no place to rest.

The keeper

Minos, the infernal judge, listens to each soul's confession and coils his tail to indicate the circle it must enter.

Dante's encounter

Francesca recounts how reading a romance together led to her first kiss with Paolo. Their story moves Dante to pity.
