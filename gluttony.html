III

Circle III: Gluttony

The insatiable, the greedy, the overfed

In the third circle, souls lie in a foul, muddy swamp beneath a perpetual storm of cold rain and filth. Cerberus prowls among them.

The souls

Gluttons are those who gave appetite mastery over reason, indulging in appetite without restraint and turning consumption into a way of life.

Their punishment

They lie in a muddy slough while a cold rain of foul, putrid waste falls on them from above, forcing them to wallow in the debris of their own excess.

The keeper

Cerberus, the three-headed hound of Hell, tears at the gluttons and tears their flesh as they grovel in the mire, a fitting guardian for appetite gone wild.

Ciacco of Florence

Dante meets the glutton known as Ciacco, whose name means “pig.” Ciacco asks Dante about Florence and predicts conflict between the city's political factions.

Knowledge after death

Ciacco tells Dante that souls in Hell can know events after their own deaths. However, once the Last Judgment arrives, they will lose the ability to perceive the future.
