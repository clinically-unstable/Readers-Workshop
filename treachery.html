IX

Circle IX: Treachery

At Hell's deepest point, betrayers are trapped in the frozen lake of Cocytus. Ice covers the lake and fixes the souls in different positions.

The depth of each soul's betrayal determines how it is fixed in the ice. Some lie with only their faces exposed; others are completely encased.

Caina & Antenora

Caina: Those who betrayed their family. Sinners are trapped up to their necks in the ice, facing slightly forwards so their tears may freeze without sealing their eyes completely.

Antenora: Those who betrayed their country. Sinners are frozen up to their necks, unable to turn their heads.

Ptolomea & Judecca

Ptolomea: Those who betrayed their guests. Sinners have even their eyes frozen shut, denied even the comfort of weeping.

Judecca: Those who betrayed their benefactors. Sinners are completely frozen, contorted into every conceivable position.

Lucifer

At the center, Lucifer is frozen waist-deep in the lake. The beating of his wings creates the icy winds that keep Cocytus frozen. His three mouths gnaw Judas Iscariot, Brutus, and Cassius—figures Dante associates with betrayal of divine and political authority.

The way out

Dante and Virgil climb down Lucifer's body and pass the center of the Earth. At the center point, gravity inverts, and from there they begin their ascent toward the surface.

They end up on the surface of the Earth on Easter Sunday, right at the base of Mount Purgatory.
